# Python-Data-Analysis-Projects

This repository contains two end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis projects built using Python.  
The objective of this repository is to demonstrate practical data analysis skills, visualization techniques, and business insight extraction.

---

**Projects Included**

**Customer Churn Analysis**

**Project Overview**
This project analyzes a telecom customer dataset to identify key factors influencing customer churn. The analysis focuses on understanding customer behavior and recognizing patterns associated with customer attrition.

**Analysis Performed**
- Data Cleaning and Preprocessing  
- Univariate and Bivariate Analysis  
- Tenure vs Churn Analysis  
- Contract Type vs Churn  
- Payment Method vs Churn  
- Service Features Impact on Churn  
- Monthly Charges Distribution  

**Key Insights**
- Customers on month-to-month contracts show higher churn tendency.  
- Customers with lower tenure are more likely to churn.  
- Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn probability.  
- Certain payment methods exhibit relatively higher churn rates.  
- Lack of value-added services increases churn risk.  

**Tools Used**
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn  

---

**Sales Data Analysis**

**Project Overview**
This project performs exploratory analysis on a retail superstore dataset to evaluate sales trends, profitability, and product performance across categories and customer segments.

**Analysis Performed**
- Data Cleaning and Preprocessing  
- Monthly Sales Trend Analysis  
- Category-wise Sales Evaluation  
- Sub-Category Performance Study  
- Monthly Profit Analysis  
- Segment-wise Sales and Profit Comparison  
- Sales-to-Profit Ratio Analysis  

**Key Insights**
- Sales and profit show strong growth during year-end months.  
- Technology category contributes the highest revenue and profit.  
- Revenue is concentrated in a few major sub-categories such as Phones and Chairs.  
- Consumer segment leads overall sales and profit contribution.  
- Profit margins vary across segments, indicating pricing and discount impact.  

**Tools Used**
Python, Pandas, Plotly
