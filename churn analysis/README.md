**Customer Churn Analysis**

**Project Overview**
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis on a telecom customer dataset to identify key factors influencing customer churn.

**Objective**
To analyze customer behavior and understand patterns associated with churn.

**Analysis Performed**
- Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
- Univariate Analysis
- Bivariate Analysis
- Tenure Group vs Churn
- Contract Type vs Churn
- Payment Method vs Churn
- Service Features vs Churn
- Monthly Charges Distribution

**Key Findings**
1. Customers on month-to-month contracts show higher churn tendency.
2. Customers with lower tenure exhibit higher churn behavior.
3. Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn.
4. Electronic check payment method shows relatively higher churn.
5. Customers without certain service features tend to churn more.

**Conclusion**
The exploratory analysis reveals that contract type, tenure, billing charges, and selected service features play a significant role in customer churn. Early-stage customers and those on flexible contracts exhibit higher churn tendencies, highlighting the importance of targeted retention strategies.
